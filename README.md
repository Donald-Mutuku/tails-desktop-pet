# Tails colonizes your desktop
Peaceful orange creature that runs across your desktop and steals your cursor sometimes.

<a href="https://boredon.itch.io/tails-colonizes-your-desktop"><img width="633" height="187" alt="Frame 84" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/2646766b-8e1e-4882-a51e-bf5d21b05628"/></a>

## Features
1. Run. He run.
2. You can drag him around
3. He sleeps when he's bored
4. Double-click to make him do a cute little dance
5. Once in a while, he will nab your cursor and you will do nothing about it

## Tech stack
1. Godot
2. My mom's hand-me-down laptop
3. Hopes and dreams
4. Cold coffee
